| section |
|---|---|
| Why this should not lose |

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | The divisor is a compile-time constant (3); both compilers replace sdiv with a magic-multiply sequence. |
| 2 | Idol's fix3 division (algo=hd10-signed-division, emit=smulh,addsub,asr,lsrcorr, magichoist=prologue) is a longer instruction sequence than gcc's for this divisor. |
| 3 | The +5 add and loop overhead are identical in shape; the gap is almost entirely the division sequence. |
