## The three ports, and what porting found

| Zig | Duo |
|---|---|
| `lua_superset_corpus.zig` | `scripts/luahost.duo` + `examples/luahost/*.duo` |
| `pass7_contract_tests.zig` + `pass7_explain_tests.zig` | `scripts/explain.duo` |

Both gates run inside `zig build agent-smoke`, assert through the **shipping
CLI**, and carry **four controls each** where the Zig originals carried none.
Both were positive-controlled by breaking a fixture and confirming red.

Porting is where the findings were:

1. **`examples/pass7/explain_smoke.duo` was broken and tracked.** It opened with
   a C-style `//` comment, which the duon lexer refuses. Nothing noticed for as
   long as it existed, because the Zig test that owned its content carried a
   byte-identical copy as a *string literal* and never opened the file. This is
   GAP-089's shape again — a duplicate cannot guard its original — one level up:
   the duplicate was of the FIXTURE, not the algorithm. Marker repaired.
2. **`duo explain` does not diagnose that failure.** It prints a raw Zig stack
   trace (`error: UnexpectedChar` + frame addresses) and exits 1. **H-8 output
   totality finding**: an emission must be graph data rendered through the role
   taxonomy, and a stack trace names no edge.
3. **`@noalloc` is not enforced anywhere a user stands.** `duo check`,
   `duo compile` and `duo explain` **all exit 0** on a module that calls
   `mem.alloc` inside a `@noalloc` function. The rejection exists only as a line
   of JSON. The deleted Zig test asserted `error.NoAllocViolation` from
   `explain_pipeline.runForProvenance`, which no CLI verb reaches — so for the
   whole life of that test the contract was green in the suite and absent in the
   product.

## The one move that unblocks five of them

The six "in-process state" rows are **one blocker wearing six hats**, and
`duo explain` already proves the pattern: it takes internal structures —
knowledge snapshot, optimization outcomes, assumptions, realizations, repair
candidates — and prints them as JSON. That is exactly why the two pass7 files
could be ported today and the others could not.

**A verb (or flags on `explain`) that also prints the transform-provenance
registry, the SIM snapshot, and the realization/cache state unblocks
`meta_transform_tests`, `call_transform_tests`, `pass6_dispatch_tests`,
`pass5_golden_tests` and both `pass8_*` files at once** — about 1,000 lines of
Zig — with **no compiler semantics changed**. It is `main.zig` work, not test
work, and it is the highest-leverage next move on this axis.

It is also H-8 aligned rather than merely convenient: making internal state a
rendered projection is what output totality asks for anyway. The gates fall out
of the law being obeyed.
