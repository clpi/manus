# GAP-233 — one diagnostic identity is carrying four causes

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** !2026-08-25T06:20:00Z
**Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** diagnostic_identity
**Filed by:** claude-reconcile
**Files:** src/dnir_lower.zig,src/main.zig,gate/application.sh

## The measurement

`idol compile <subject> --backend=c --emit=c` over `examples/`, x86_64-linux-gnu.
Of 253 subjects, 210 pass `idol check`; of those, 141 refuse with
`missing-application-id` and 13 realize.

Until `5f74ee27` the C arm printed only the note, so all 141 reached a reader as
the same six words. `refuseApplication` had been binding `diagnostic.relation`
from `applicationFace` the whole time and the renderer discarded it — the arm
twenty lines below it, for `c_backend.emitSource`, already printed its own
`in relation:`. With the face printed, 141 separates into four:

    63  compiler intrinsic (`__*`)   __emit 10, __comptimematch 6, __c_call 5,
                                     __static_assert 4, __comptimemap 3, and
                                     ~20 more, each 1–2
    43  host relation, no entity     new 9, assert 5, setmetatable 4, type 2,
                                     cast 2, typeof, sqrt, random, …
    20  no face bound at all         curried `f(a)(b)`; see the correction below
    15  non-callable subject applied tt 11, u, v4f64 3

60 distinct faces in total.

**MEMBERSHIP CORRECTED, and the total is a coincidence.** `v4f64` is NOT a
non-callable subject: the three subjects spell `simd.v4f64(1.0, …)`, `simd` is a
builtin global (`src/sema.zig:2861`), and both sema (`src/sema.zig:5029`) and
codegen (`src/codegen.zig:23138`) already treat it as a constructor. The face
reads `v4f64` only because it is the last dotted segment. Those 3 belong with
the intrinsic/builtin family, taking it to 66.

Three subjects were missed by the face grep and belong here instead:
`examples/call_index_assign.id` (face `arr` — the file this gap's own acceptance
#2 names), `examples/mem_demo.id` (`self._bufs(...)`), and
`examples/json_direct_iteration_smoke.id` (`decoded_arr(1)`). So the family is
still 15, with three out and three in. The arithmetic coincidence is why
acceptance #4 asks for a runner: a hand-derived membership survived a recount
that it should not have.

## Why this is a defect and not a report

**`law.fact.producer.one` in the diagnostic layer.** One identity that four
causes reach is not an identity; it is a bucket. A reader who greps
`missing-application-id` cannot tell which question was asked, and a gate that
pins the count cannot tell which one it caught — the same sentence
`a59c8ef9` used when it renamed `result-pack-arity` because two producers
spelled one note.

Each family wants a different answer:

- **compiler intrinsic** — `__emit`, `__c_call`, `__static_assert` and the
  `__comptime*` set are compile-time faces. Whether they should have relation
  entities at all is a question for that owner, and `GAP-226`'s acceptance does
  not cover them. They are the LARGEST family and were being read as that gap.
- **host relation with no entity** — this is `GAP-226` proper, and it is 43 of
  141, not 141.
- **no face bound** — the refusal reaches `invalidGraphFacts` with no occurrence
  to attribute. That is either a scan that found nothing to bind or a consumer
  refusing without one; the two are not distinguishable from the outside today.
- **non-callable subject applied** — `examples/call_index_module.id` is

      tt: [4]i64 = {1, 2, 3, 4}
      tt(2)

  which `law.md` §5 rules in one sentence ("`()` is ordinary application. It
  never means table indexing") and `gate/application.sh:333` already names as a
  NEGATIVE probe. It passes `idol check` and then refuses here as a missing id.
  A correct refusal wearing the wrong cause is worse than a wrong answer,
  because the ratchet that counts it will move when the wall is repaired and
  nothing about this subject changed.

## Three sites, but ONE producer — corrected

`grep -n '"missing-application-id"' src/dnir_lower.zig` returns three non-test
sites (169, 2954, 2955), and this gap first read the `no face bound` family as
the third of them. **That was wrong, and the measurement is decisive.**

All 141 come from **2954** alone. Two independent facts settle it:

- every one of the 141 prints an application id, which 2955 cannot produce
  because it binds nothing; and
- for all 141, the printed id equals `blocking_applications[0]` from
  `idol graph <f>` — exactly `OccurrenceBridge.first_unresolved`. 141/141, no
  exceptions.

169 is unreachable for this corpus: the guard at 2946
(`if (!graph.gateTransportModule() and occurrences.unresolved != 0)`) refuses
before `lowerModuleFromGraph` is ever called, and no `examples/*.id` is
gate-transport. So this file is NOT a `law.fact.producer.one` instance for this
note. The other two sites exist and may be reachable from other corpora; nothing
here measured that.

**The real cause of the missing face was `applicationFace` not recursing.** A
curried `f(a)(b)` is a `.call` whose callee is itself a `.call`, and both
`exprFace` and `applicationFace`'s AST tail answered only `.name` and `.field`,
falling to `node.name` — null, because an indirect-callee node carries no name.
17 of the 20 were literally `to(str)(x)`, the operation-first face `CLAUDE.md`
records as migratable debt; the rest were `greet("Duo")(…)` and
`concat("Hello")(…)`. One construct, one repair. Both faces walk to the chain
head now, and the 20 answer `to`, `greet`, `concat`.

## Its effect on GAP-226's own ranking

The wall's site census ranks `host-relation-no-entity` +
`host-relation-name-declared-in-home` at 74% of unpublished applications. That
is a count of SITES. This is a count of SUBJECTS THAT CANNOT BE REALIZED, and
the ranking inverts: intrinsics 63, wall 43. Both are true and they answer
different questions — one program is refused by its first unresolved occurrence,
so a subject is attributed to whichever cause it meets first, not to the cause
with the most sites in it.

Neither number is wrong and neither is the other's substitute. Recording that
here because the two were about to be read as one.

## Acceptance #2 is answered: `check` holds the descriptor and does not ask

`idol graph examples/call_index_module.id` publishes both halves in one graph —
node 2 is `tt` and the aggregate row carries
`"descriptor":{"kind":"array","extent":4,"element":{"kind":"i64"}}` — while node
8 is the `.call`. Nothing joins them, and the join point is three lines wide:

    src/sema.zig:4967   const ft = try self.check_expr(c.func);   // descriptor in hand
    src/sema.zig:4968   if (c.form == .braced and ft == .func)    // the MIRROR guard,
                                                                 // c0 §43 law.brace
    src/sema.zig:5094   const result: RT = switch (ft) {
                            .func => |f| f.ret.*,
                            else => .any,                        // .array falls here
                        };

So the dual of an existing guard is simply unwritten: `law.brace` refuses a
CALLABLE subject wearing BRACES, and `law.md` §5's rule — a DESCRIPTOR subject
wearing PARENS — has no guard. That sema uses this same descriptor elsewhere is
proven by the `.index` arm at `src/sema.zig:4656`
(`if (ot == .array) return ot.array.elem.*;`): `tt[2]` reads it, `tt(2)` drops it.

Bounded honestly: the descriptor is available for a BARE-NAME callee (`tt`,
`arr`). For `self._bufs(...)` and `json.decode(...)` `check_expr` returns `.any`
(`src/sema.zig:4626`), so two of the fifteen are the family by shape without
sema holding their descriptor.

**A GATE PINS THE CURRENT BEHAVIOUR AND MUST MOVE WITH THE REPAIR.**
`gate/architecture-companion.sh:98-113` asserts
`CHECK-NOT-DIRECT-ADMISSION: call_index_assign.id must pass idol check (sema
path)` — it requires `check` to exit 0 on a subject law says is ill-formed.
Whoever writes the guard changes that gate in the same commit, or lands a green
tree that contradicts itself. Recording it here because it is invisible from the
sema side.

Four producers already rule `()`-is-not-indexing — `docs/spec/law.md:152`,
`src/place.zig` (pinned by `test "place: an ordinary call is not an indexed
read"`), `gate/delimiter-projection-law.sh`, and `gate/callfold.sh` — and sema
is the one that does not.

## Acceptance

1. Each family refuses under its own note, minted where the cause is known —
   at minimum `non-callable-subject` separated from `missing-application-id`,
   since law already rules it and a gate already probes it.
2. `examples/call_index_module.id` and `examples/call_index_assign.id` refuse
   with the non-callable note, and `idol check` is the arm that decides whether
   they should have been accepted at all.
3. The `no face bound` family either binds one or says why it cannot, so that
   20 subjects stop reporting a cause nobody can act on.
4. The four-way split is recomputed by a runner rather than pinned here. The
   loop that produced it is in `gaps/GAP-226.md`; the face is now available in
   the refusal output, so classifying is `grep 'in relation:'`.
