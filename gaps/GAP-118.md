# GAP-118 — Environment lookup erases absence into empty string

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** !2026-08-09T20:08:27Z
**Priority:** P0 · **Kind:** expressiveness_gap
**Filed by:** codex_semantic_normalization
**Files:** lib/std/os.duo,lib/std/script.duo,docs/spec/constitution.duo

The authoritative vocabulary cannot currently express environment lookup while preserving absent versus present empty string. `lib/std/os.duo` maps both missing and present-empty to `""`; `std.script.getenv(name, fallback)` then bakes defaulting into a namespace API. Required audit: name/key subject, environment world authority, absent|present(value) structured result, conversion/default composition, build-stage folding, and structured canonical diagnostic. Do not invent a replacement relation spelling until this family is admitted.
