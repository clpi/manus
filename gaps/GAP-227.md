## What closed on this lane

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | sh gate/world/stage.sh |

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | Seven sections, non-vacuous (§1 fails against the pre-repair precheck, verified by planting it). |

- **Acceptance 1 — DONE.** `expr@{ stage = compile }` compiles, runs, and
  answers what `@(expr)` answers, compared over the same subject.
- **Acceptance 2 — STRUCTURAL, not yet a graph fact.** The two spellings are ONE
  AST node; `unop.world_face` is provenance and the printer is its only reader,
  per `law.md` §9. So the ruling's `==` cannot drift. What is still missing is
  the graph half: no derived-world identity is published, because `WorldFact`
  still has no parent and no delta range.
- **Acceptance 3 — DONE, on running programs.** `5@{}` is `5`, and `x@{}` keeps
  a runtime `x` runtime, which is the half that makes it the identity rather
  than a stage change. `(1+2)@{ stage = compile }@{ stage = compile }` is `3`,
  and the reprint carries the fact ONCE — idempotence is applied at formation,
  so it cannot drift either.
- **Acceptance 4 — DONE.** `@(cwd())` refuses `compile-stage-absent`, with a
  control proving `cwd()` itself runs. A value absent at the stage is absent
  from that world.
- **Acceptance 5 — DONE.** `@{ tax = 1 }` names the missing fact, a non-compile
  stage names the missing evaluator, a positional entry names no fact, and a
  duplicate member in one literal is an error.

## What remains

1. `WorldFact` gains `parent` and a fact-delta range, and the compile-stage
   world becomes an entity whose parent is the module's ambient world — which
   must also become an entity, since worlds are published per reached host home
   today and the CURRENT world of a module is not one.
2. Both spellings then publish the SAME derived-world id, and every `@( … )`
   occurrence in a module draws that one world — hash-consed on
   (parent, delta set), which is what makes acceptance 3's idempotence an
   identity property of the graph and not only of the syntax.
3. The fold is licensed BY the world rather than beside it:
   `dnir_lower.compileStageValue` asks `comptime_eval` directly today, and the
   honest architecture has it asking the compile-stage world for the value and
   getting the no-fallthrough refusal from the world's own roster.

| # | directive |
