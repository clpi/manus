**Status:** OPEN · **Priority:** P0 · **Kind:** architecture
**Owner:** Cursor / path convergence

## Obligation

`scripts/` violates `law.singular.one` (C0, total semantic closure §3). Plurality
encodes cardinality, not identity. The root is migration debt — **not** repaired
by renaming to `script/`.

gate controls, docs). `lib/semantic/*` is deleted (`law.catalog.zero`) — do
not rehome anything into a relation catalog.

## Gate
