# GAP-041 — the 46 ARM64 encoders are named `encode_add_reg`, which LAW-ONE denies

**Filed** 2026-08-08. **Status** open. **Cost** +338 deny lines across 13
`audit100` rows, forcing a rebaseline.

## What happened

`lib/std/compiler/arm64.duo` went from 9 encoders to 46 by lifting what
`ext/ward/src/wasm/jit_arm64.duo` already had — immediates, loads/stores, frame
ops, compare/select, branches, div/shifts, width conversion. That work is
correct and differentially proven: `lib/std/compiler/arm64check.duo` assembles
all 50 cases with `clang`, compares word for word, and agreed 50/50 on the
first run.

It is also **new deny debt**, and the rebaseline says so rather than hiding it:

    arm64check.duo (173 lines)   56 snake · 68 underprefix · 75 upper
    arm64.duo                    37 new `encode_*` functions

Every `encode_add_reg` is three LAW-ONE violations at once — snake case, an
axis name (`_reg` is the operand kind, which §0 rule 1 says moves to a LEVEL),
and `encode_` is a `compute_`-family accessor prefix.

## Why it was not fixed in the same commit

The agent that wrote it flagged this and declined, correctly: three example
proofs call those names by hand (`examples/pass16_arm64_proof.duo`,
`pass16_arm64_exec_proof.duo`, `pass16_macho_proof.duo`) and it did not own
`examples/`. Renaming half the pair breaks the gate that proves the encoders
work.

## The canonical shape

Not a mechanical rename. §0 rule 1 says the qualifier moves to a LEVEL, and §6
says methods travel with values, so the target is roughly:

    encode_add_reg(d, n, m)     ->   arm64:add(reg)(d, n, m)
    encode_ldr_imm(t, n, off)   ->   arm64:ldr(imm)(t, n, off)
    encode_movz_shift(d, v, s)  ->   arm64:movz(shift)(d, v, s)

i.e. one `add` edge with an operand-kind LEVEL, not 46 flat names. That is the
ONE EDGE reduction the audit asks for, and doing it properly collapses the
count rather than renaming 46 things to 46 other things.

## Repair sequence

1. Introduce the levelled surface alongside the flat names.
2. Repoint `arm64check.duo` (owns all 50 cases — it is the safety net).
3. Repoint the three example proofs.
4. Delete the flat names; lower the `snake`/`underprefix`/`accessor` budgets in
   `scripts/audit100.duo` in the same commit, which is that gate's protocol.

Step 4 is the point. Until it lands, `audit100`'s budgets carry this debt
openly.
