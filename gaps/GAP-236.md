## The ruling (owner ruling, 2026-08-25)

Recorded here as filed law; the wording below condenses the owner's ruling of
2026-08-25 and is authoritative for this gap's acceptance surface.

**A. The proposed `(:)` anonymous-subject parameter is REJECTED.** The existing
faces already cover the space with zero new syntax. The acceptance surface is
this five-case declaration matrix:

| # | Face | Meaning under the ruling |
|---|------|--------------------------|
| 1 | `f = (x)` | ordinary callable input |
| 2 | `r = (x, y)` | generic relation; RESOLUTION establishes the subject where semantically warranted |
| 3 | `x:r = (y)` | relation `r` with explicit subject specialization `x`; the body's leading `:relation()` sections use the ambient subject; declaration face ↔ invocation face symmetry: `x:r = (…)` ↔ `x:r(…)` |
| 4 | `x: { r = (y) … }` | the same specialization compressed by a subject home — the home supplies the ambient subject to every declaration inside |
| 5 | `x.r = (y)` | STATIC member binding under `x` (dot = static projection), NOT subject specialization; `x.r(…)` applies the projected callable with no subject unless one independently resolves |

Dot and colon must never collapse: declaration location must not determine
relation identity, and a subject home compresses — never forks — the relation
(`x:r` does not mint an OO identity `x.r`). File scope is NEVER an implicit
receiver: a file declaring `add = (a, b)` implies no subject.

**B. Partial application is FACT SATURATION, never positional currying.**
Twelve frozen laws:

1. No curry kingdom — no new runtime model; only application, relation
   values, packs, demand, capture.
2. No automatic positional currying — fewer arguments is not an implicit
   lambda.
3. A partially established relation carries exact supplied facts and exact
   open requirements.
4. Subject is a role, never mechanically argument zero.
5. Specialization may leave the subject open — `mass(kg)` is a relation value
   with the unit supplied and the subject open ONLY when relation law makes
   the roles unique (the existing `to(str)` / `xs:map(to(str))` pattern).
6. No first-return chaining — result selection only through exact pack
   demand/correspondence (PACK-ONE).
7. Descriptor/world/stage facts participate directly, not as disguised
   positional arguments.
8. Capture does not imply allocation — physical closure representation is
   demand/escape-selected.
9. Equivalent faces converge — `r(x, a)` and `x:r(a)` are one application
   semantics once resolution proves it.
10. Homes compress subjects.
11. Static relation sections beat trivial lambdas (`map(to(str))`,
    `map(.name)` where admitted).
12. Ambiguity fails closed — never guess which role a supplied value fills.

Four look-alikes stay distinguished: subject-open specialization; semantic
specialization levels (`to(str)(u32) = digits` on the declaration face —
already CLAUDE.md's `call.face`); ordinary callable result (`make(a)(b)` where
`make` answers a callable); captured partial application (a binding holding a
specialized relation value). Additionally: world requirements never silently
become closure captures (explicit lexical grant only), and descriptor `{}`
application never inherits callable currying.

**Addendum (owner ruling, 2026-08-25).** Currying is handled canonically by
the subject-qualified specialized declaration face — `body:weight(g) = (…)`.
This is the SAME declaration algebra `call.face` already establishes for
`to(str) = (value)` and `read(number) = (lx, b)`: parenthesized packs on the
declaration head are PROJECTION/SPECIALIZATION levels — saturation facts —
the subject face sits left of the colon, and the declaration face mirrors the
invocation face (`body:weight(g) = …` ↔ `x:weight(g)` / `body:weight(g)`).
Consequences: (a) no separate curry mechanism or curry kingdom exists — the
declaration face IS the canonical spelling of progressive fact saturation;
(b) which head pack is projection vs operand is resolved by relation law and
the saturation model (laws 1–12), never by pack count or position; (c) the
matrix gains a sixth case:

| # | Face | Meaning under the ruling |
|---|------|--------------------------|
| 6 | `x:r(k) = (y)` | relation `r`, subject specialization `x`, projection/saturation fact `k`, operand `y` |

**Case 1 — SATISFIES.** `double = (x: i64): i64` with `double(21)` checks
clean and publishes the func entity and a complete application fact with a
subject slot. Pin: `sema: gap236 case1`.

**Case 2 — SATISFIES, in both directions.** With `mass` declared, an untyped
param body `body:mass() * factor` resolves and checks clean — resolution
establishes the subject. With `mass` undeclared the same body refuses with
exactly:

    error: 'mass' is neither a descriptor nor a callable, so the
    receiver_parenthesized application has no subject: descriptor space holds
    no 'mass' and callable space holds no 'mass' (no declaration, no builtin,
    no foreign import)

(producer `src/sema.zig` `checkMethodCall` refusal, the message minted at the
two `is neither a descriptor nor a callable` sites). Fails closed — law (12).
Pins: `sema: gap236 case2` (both).

**Case 3 — MIS-RESOLVES; symmetry broken.** `body:weight = (factor)` at file
scope is accepted by check, and then:

- The parser (`try_parse_qualified_func_assign`, `src/parser.zig:4877`)
  reads it as a func_decl with `path = {body, weight}`, `method = true`, and
  `ensure_implicit_self_param` (`src/parser.zig:4844`) prepends an untyped
  receiver parameter named `self` — the Lua method face, not a subject
  specialization of relation `weight`.
- Sema files the name under `table_methods` (`src/sema.zig`, the
  `fd.path.len >= 2 and fd.method` arm) and defines nothing in scope.
- `idol graph` publishes NOTHING: one module node, zero func nodes, zero
  applications, zero callable linkages, fact coverage all zero. The subject
  specialization does not exist as a graph fact; the declaration is
  semantically inert.
- The ruling's symmetric invocation `body:weight(3)` then refuses with the
  same "'weight' is neither a descriptor nor a callable" diagnostic — the
  declaration face minted no callable the invocation face can see. Measured
  identically when the subject is a declared descriptor (`body: { m: i64 }`
  first): still no entity, still refused.

This is simultaneously the dot/colon collapse the ruling forbids (colon
declaration minting a dotted path identity) and a silent-fallback violation
(`law.fallback.zero`): accepted, inert, unreported. Pins: `sema: gap236
case3` (both).

**Case 4 — SATISFIES the acceptance shape.** `body: { weight = (factor) … }`
routes through `parse_descriptor_slot` (`src/parser.zig:1131`): the relation
keeps its single-word identity `weight`, the home types the prepended
receiver param as `body`, and the hoisted declaration is an ordinary
func_decl. `b: body = {}` then `b:weight(3)` checks clean and PUBLISHES an
application fact — relation resolved, subject and operand in their roles,
target cardinality one. The home compresses; it does not fork. Residue noted
below: the compression is realized as a synthesized receiver parameter, not
as an ambient-subject fact, and the hoisted relation lands at module scope
with no home-membership fact. Pin: `sema: gap236 case4`.

**Case 5 — MIS-RESOLVES (silently inert), invocation half right.**
`body.weight = (factor)` parses as func_decl `path = {body, weight}`,
`method = false` (same producer as case 3, field arm). Sema's declaration
registration covers `path.len == 1 and !method` (scope define) and
`path.len >= 2 and method` (table_methods); this shape falls between and is
registered NOWHERE. Check accepts it; graph publishes no func node; the
invocation `body.weight(3)` is left unresolved-and-blocking (visible in
`idol graph` `blocking_applications`) yet `idol check` still exits 0. The
ruling's static-member reading — a projected callable with no subject — is
not implemented; nothing at all is. Pin: `sema: gap236 case5`.

**Case 6 — REFUSES at parse, in every spelling; fails closed.**
`body:weight(g) = (factor)` at file scope refuses with `expected expression,
got '='` at the `=`. The mechanism: `try_parse_qualified_func_assign`
(`src/parser.zig:4877`) bails when a head pack intervenes between the
relation name and `=` (the `.assign` requirement at `src/parser.zig:4935`
sees `(`); the level-edge reader accepts only unqualified single-segment
heads (`src/parser.zig:2877`, `path.items.len != 1` returns null); the
statement then falls into the typed-binding/expression route and refuses at
the `=` (producer `src/parser.zig:6261`). The home-compressed spellings
refuse too: braced `body: { weight(g) = (…) }` with `expected descriptor
field name or spread` (`src/parser.zig:4823` — the head pack collides with
the case-payload face of descriptor tables), and the offside home with the
same `expected expression, got '='`. The closest working faces today are the
UNQUALIFIED level edge `weight(g) = (…)` (minting `weight__g` via
`relation_edge_symbol`) plus the subject-first invocation
`b:weight(g)(...)` — declaration face ↔ invocation face symmetry does not
yet extend the subject face to the declaration head. No mis-resolution: the
face is simply not admitted. Pin: `sema: gap236 case6`.

**Invocation symmetry across faces.** `scale(2, 3)` and `2:scale(3)` against
one declared relation publish the SAME application shape (one relation, one
subject, one operand, target cardinality one) — law (9) holds today,
producer `subjectSlotArgs` / SLOT-ROLE-ONE. Pin: `sema: gap236 law9`.

## Residue — exact, with owners; no semantics changed this increment

1. **Case 3 producer fork.** `try_parse_qualified_func_assign`
   (`src/parser.zig:4877`) turns `x:r = (y)` into the Lua method face.
   Owner: parser + the `table_methods` arm in sema. The repair the ruling
   demands — one relation identity `r` with a subject-specialization fact on
   `x`, symmetric with `x:r(…)` — is NOT a small mis-resolution fix (it needs
   a subject-specialization fact the graph does not yet carry), so it was not
   attempted blind.
2. **Case 5 falls between registration arms.** func_decl with two-segment
   path and `method = false` registers nowhere; accepted and inert. Owner:
   sema declaration registration. Smallest lawful move is to fail closed or
   to bind the projected callable; deciding which is the ruling's static-
   member semantics, deferred with the case-3 repair.
3. **Under-saturated application published as complete.**
   `subjectSlotArgs` + the call-check path admit `add(1)` with no open-
   requirements fact (laws (3)/(12)). Owner: sema application recording.
4. **Projection identity is a name mangle.** `relation_edge_symbol` mints
   `to__str`; no projection pack fact (`law.projection.pack`).
5. **Case 4 compression is a synthesized receiver param** typed by the home,
   hoisted to module scope with no home-membership fact — compression works
   but the ambient subject is positional, and two homes declaring the same
   relation word meet at module scope as overloads rather than as homed
   relations.
6. **`idol check` exits 0 over blocking applications** (case 5's invocation)
   — silence at the check boundary over a fact the graph already marks
   blocking.
7. **Case 6 is unimplemented.** The subject-qualified level declaration
   `x:r(k) = (y)` refuses in all three spellings (file scope, braced home,
   offside home). Owners: `try_parse_qualified_func_assign` +
   `parse_level_edge` (the two readers that each carry half of the face),
   and the descriptor-table case-payload collision for the braced home.
   Admitting it must keep the addendum's law: the head pack is a
   projection/saturation fact resolved by relation law, never by pack count
   or position — so the repair is a graph fact, not a third name-mangle,
   and it was not attempted blind this increment.
