decomposition, and forbids underscores, compounds, and numeric taxonomy suffixes.
Current tracked paths violate this at scale — PATH-ONE is constitutional prose
without enforcement.

| Class | Action |
|---|---|
| semantic home | decompose into real home hierarchy |
| tool/workflow label | relocate outside canonical teaching space or one irreducible operational name |
| history/bootstrap | classify mechanically; ratchet down |
| obsolete | delete |

**New-path violations = 0** immediately (`gates/path.id` on staged added/renamed paths).
