# GAP-078 — grammar-role projections must come from one executable authority

**Opened** 2026-08-08 · **Status OPEN — re-audited 2026-08-17.**

Law: source law selects one projection from the one grammar authority; editor
roles are generated projections and ordinary access remains application.

Today: the duplicate in-tree LSP, handwritten role taxonomy, legend generator,
raw scanner, highlight corpus, and their build/doctor hooks were deleted on
2026-08-17. Durable language intelligence is the sibling `idol-native` LSP.
No historical highlight result is current admission evidence.

Delta: semantic tokens wait for compiler-published graph-owned source spans and
the one grammar authority's generated role projection in the durable sibling
LSP. Add positive and negative controls there when those facts exist. Do not
port or restore the old taxonomy, brackets, retired `..`, raw scanner, corpus,
or a C fallback.

## The line where they give different answers

`fixtures/highlight/copula.id` line 3:

```
count:u32
```

It is TIGHT and it has NO call group. R1 says **copula**; spacing says
**invoke**. `fixtures/highlight/copula.roles.id` — hand-written — says
`copula`, and `docs/spec/roles.md` §5.1 carries the argument in full:

> R1 is the parse rule and is total. §0d is a statement about what canonical
> layout emits, and it is a *corollary* on canonical input, not the decider.

`highlight.id`'s own header takes the other side and says so out loud:

> `:` by LAYOUT … Because the renderer is normative, spacing is a FACT ABOUT
> THE GRAPH that survives into the byte stream.

Both arguments are serious. Neither is a mistake. **One of them is law and the
other is a finding, and nothing in the tree says which.**

## Why it cannot be left

§0d: "`highlights.scm` and the LSP legend are GENERATED from the graph service
— hand edits are an H-1 drift finding." There are now two generators for one
legend. Whichever a front end picks, the other convicts it, and both corpora
are golden. G-TOTAL's coverage and ambiguity numbers are not comparable across
them: 17-with-`ambiguous` and 18-without measure different things.

## What the LSP does today, stated so it is not mistaken for a ruling

`tools/lsp/src/server.id` serves `textDocument/semanticTokens` with a legend
GENERATED by `tools/lsp/legend.id` from **`docs/spec/roles.md` §2 + §3**, and
its scanner decides `:` by **R1**. That was written before `highlight.id`
landed, and it was anchored to `docs/spec/` because that is where the tracked
spec lives — not because the question was settled. `zig build lsp-gate` asserts
the token stream for `copula.id` byte for byte against the hand-written
sidecar, so the LSP is currently CONVICTABLE by one corpus and silent about the
other.

## What the 2026-08-08 record required

A ruling in `CLAUDE.md` that names, for the record:

1. the count — 17 or 18 — and, if 18, the eighteenth member by name;
2. the decider for `:` — R1 or spacing — and what happens to `count:u32`;
3. whether the §0g fine splits are roles, cards, or modifiers;
4. which of `fixtures/highlight/*.roles.id` and `fixtures/highlight/idol/**`
   is the golden corpus, or how both are.

Then ONE generator survives, the other's front end regenerates from it, and
both gates assert against the same corpus. Until then any G-TOTAL percentage
published from either side should carry which taxonomy produced it.

## Falsifier

If a later commit reports a G-TOTAL coverage number without naming its
taxonomy, or if `role-scan` and the LSP's own census are compared to each other
as if they measured the same quantity, this gap was closed by forgetting rather
than by ruling.

## RULING 2026-08-08 — both questions were already decided; this is the mapping

**Owner: resolved. An agent may now execute this mechanically. Do not reopen
either question; implement the table.**

### Q1 — `:` copula vs invoke. **R1 WINS. `roles.md` is right.**

Already ruled in `CLAUDE.md` §0d: *"grammar.md R1 decides the `:` role; §0d's
spacing sentence only RENDERS it."* `docs/spec/grammar.md` is NORMATIVE (Pass
108). §0d's sentence — "canonical layout makes L1 lexical" — is a statement
about what the formatter EMITS on canonical input, which is a corollary, not a
decider. `count:u32` is a **copula**.

`highlight.id`'s counter-argument ("the renderer is normative, so spacing is a
fact about the graph that survives into the byte stream") is serious and it is
still WRONG, for a reason worth keeping: spacing survives into the byte stream
only for input the formatter has already touched. A role function that is total
must answer for input it has NOT touched — hand-typed, mid-edit, pasted, or
foreign. R1 answers there; spacing does not. **Totality is what breaks the tie.**

### Q2 — 17 vs 18. **NEITHER IS LAW. The count was never the ruling.**

Already corrected in `CLAUDE.md` §0d: the rendered spec states H-2 as *"the role
set is closed"* — **with no number**. "18" was a summary artifact of mine and I
adjudicated on it once before catching it. The RULING is CLOSURE: finite,
enumerated, total. The COUNT is a BINDING and is checked against what the
renderer needs.

So the 17/18 disagreement is **not a conflict to resolve** — it is two correct
enumerations of different working sets. `roles.md` §4's arithmetic showing H-2's
18 is underspecified was RIGHT. Stop defending a number in both files.

### Q3 — `name` vs `place`, and this is the one that matters

`roles.md` calls the ordinary-binding role `name` and leaves it **UNHUED**,
counting it as a hole. `highlight.id` calls it `place`, blue, heavy at
definition. **H-6 makes the role function TOTAL**, and an unhued role cannot
satisfy a total function — that is exactly why one implementation measured 20%
coverage and the other 100%.

**The hue is required; the spelling is not.** Take `place` (it is what the
spec's own renderer calls it, and a binding IS a place in dnir), hue it blue,
heavy at definition.

### The mechanical mapping — apply, do not relitigate

| question | winner | loser becomes |
|---|---|---|
| `:` copula/invoke | `roles.md` — **R1** | `highlight.id`'s spacing decider is a FINDING; delete it |
| closed count | **neither** — closure is the law | both files stop asserting a number |
| ordinary binding | `highlight.id` — **`place`, blue, heavy** | `roles.md` §3's "largest hole" is CLOSED; rewrite it as closed |
| string literal | `roles.md` — **`literal`** | a spelling difference only; no argument either way, so take the tracked spec |
| §0g fine splits | `highlight.id` — **cards within roles** | but see below: Pass 118 §4 names SPACE WALK its own ROLE, so cards are not universal |
| `ambiguous` | `roles.md` — **keep it** | G-TOTAL requires ambiguity=0 to be COUNTED, not absent; a taxonomy with no way to say "two candidates" cannot report the number the gate needs |

**Pass 118 §4 overrides the card reading where it speaks:** SPACE WALK
(`to[str]`) is its own ROLE, descriptor-tinted. Braces (matching stack), comma
(stratify), unit `*`, dnir `=`/`->` stay CARDS. Where the law names a role, take
the law; the hue discriminator governs only where it is silent.

### Acceptance — the fix is not done until all four hold

1. ONE taxonomy module. The other is deleted, not left beside it.
2. `count:u32` resolves **copula** in both corpora, proven by value at the byte.
3. Coverage 100%, ambiguity 0, **provenance 100%** — three numbers, all three
   reported. Specificity reported separately (Pass 118): a token whose card
   cannot beat its lexical class is a finding.
4. Positive-controlled: corrupt the decider and prove the gate goes red.

## CLOSED 2026-08-08 — the mapping was executed; here is what it cost and what it found

**One taxonomy module survives: `tools/lsp/src/highlight.id`.
`scripts/role_scan.id` is DELETED** (942 lines), along with the `role-scan`
build step. It was not parked beside the survivor and it was not renamed into
it: `git show` on the closing commit shows a deletion.

### The six decisions, as landed

| question | landed |
|---|---|
| `:` copula vs invoke | the SPACING decider is gone. `scan` now implements grammar.md R1: a `:` is INVOKE exactly when a name follows it immediately AND that name is immediately followed by `(`, `{` or `"`; every other `:` is the COPULA and opens a descriptor edge. That arm is TOTAL — it declines on no input — which is the property that beat spacing |
| the count | **no number is asserted anywhere.** `closed()` checks CLOSURE (non-empty, enumerated, unique names and codes, every role hued/cited/captured/carded, every card refining a real role, exactly one diagnostic member). `scripts/highlight.id` checks the legend against the taxonomy's own length instead of against 18. `tools/lsp/legend.id` refuses an EMPTY enumeration, not a wrongly-sized one |
| ordinary binding | `place`, blue, heavy — unchanged in the projector, and `docs/spec/roles.md` §5.8 is rewritten from "the largest hole" to CLOSED |
| string literal | `text` → `literal`, in the table, the cards, the tree-sitter bindings, the LSP scanner and the gate |
| §0g fine splits | cards, EXCEPT the space walk. Pass 118 §4 names SPACE WALK a role, so `retrieve` is a role (orange, cards `walk` and `index`), and `[`/`]` left `layout` |
| `ambiguous` | kept and made structural: `closed()` now REFUSES to answer unless exactly one diagnostic member exists, and removing it is a positive control below |

### The four acceptance conditions, by value

1. **ONE taxonomy module.** `scripts/role_scan.id` deleted; `docs/spec/roles.md`
   rewritten as the prose face of the surviving table rather than a rival
   enumeration; `tools/lsp/legend.id` reads that table through `hlmode=legend`.
2. **`count:u32` resolves COPULA in BOTH corpora, at the byte.**
   `fixtures/highlight/idol/copula.id:13:6` is asserted `copula`/`ascribe` and
   `:7` is asserted `descriptor`/`shape` by the proof table;
   `fixtures/highlight/copula.id` span `{ 3, 6, 1, "copula" }` is asserted by
   the hand-written sidecar. Corrupting the decider moves BOTH.
3. **coverage 100% · ambiguity 0 · provenance 100%**, published by
   `zig build highlight-corpus` over both corpora at once — 3583 of 3583
   canonical non-whitespace bytes carry a role, 0 unresolved spans outside the
   negative controls, 19 of 19 roles used answer `why()` with a rule citation
   AND a dnir edge. **SPECIFICITY is reported separately**, per Pass 118: 17 of
   86 program lexemes are EDGE-decided, 69 are spelling-decided.
4. **Positive-controlled.** Three controls, run and recorded:
   * relax the R1 test to "any tight `:` is an invoke" (the spacing reading) —
     the gate goes RED with 4 findings: the golden line, the proof at 13:6, the
     span sidecar (`scan(3 6 1 invoke)` against `sidecar(3 6 1 copula)`), and
     the proof floor. **Both corpora convict independently.**
   * delete the `ambiguous` card — the projector exits 3 with *"the taxonomy
     carries 0 diagnostic members; G-TOTAL needs exactly one"* and prints no
     roles at all.
   * add a twentieth role — `closed()` accepts it and the legend follows to 20.
     That is the proof the count is a BINDING: under the old code 19 and 20 were
     both hard failures.

   Note what the first control does NOT move: coverage stays 100%, ambiguity
   stays 0, provenance stays 100%. Re-deciding a role is not a coverage loss,
   which is why the corpora exist and why the three G-TOTAL numbers alone would
   have passed a wrong answer.

### Two defects the merge surfaced, both repaired

* `'x'` fell through to the arithmetic arm and rendered as operator / place /
  operator — a string read as two products around a variable. Only the flat
  corpus contained a single-quoted literal, which is why the byte corpus never
  saw it. grammar.md §1 gives `string` both spellings.
* the quote DELIMITER carried the layout card `group`. A card that cannot beat
  "punctuation" is the Pass 118 SPECIFICITY finding by definition; it is now
  `quote`, and the interpolation braces keep `group` because they do delimit an
  expression.

### WHERE THE RULING DID NOT REACH — the one open finding

**`|` off a descriptor edge.** §0g reads it as a PIPE ("channels = stream places
composed by `|`") and pass100 §4 reads it as `bor` over integer operands, for
which H-4 names no hue at all. The ruling settled six questions and this was not
one of them.

The projector used to answer `pipe` for every `|` that was not a union — a guess
wearing a role. It now DIAGNOSES unless a stream place was opened on the line,
which a world action does: `sh "ls" | grep("duo")` is a pipe because `sh` opened
one; `out = xs | ys` has two candidates and no rule. `fixtures/highlight/union.id`
is gated as a CONTROL for exactly that span.

**This is a finding about the LAW, not about the implementation.** It closes by
giving `bor` a hue or by writing the rule that decides operand space, and it must
not close by widening the projector. -- gap[078]

### What the LSP front end owed, and what it now carries

`tools/lsp/legend.id` regenerates the semantic-token legend from the surviving
table (19 types, 2 modifiers), `tools/lsp/src/server.id`'s scanner speaks
`literal` rather than `text` and stops asserting a count in its hover, and
`tools/lsp/gate.id` asserts the merged legend in order plus the renumbered token
array — inserting `retrieve` at index 7 shifted comment 15→16, place 11→12,
invoke 7→8 and callable 8→9. That renumber is the legend doing its job.

**Still owed, and named rather than done:** `server.id` carries its OWN role
scanner (`tools/lsp/CLAUDE.md` counts it as one of three line scanners that
exist only because the graph service answers no role queries). It agrees with
the merged taxonomy on names and on R1, but it is a second implementation of the
role FUNCTION and it does not yet carry `retrieve`, the stream rule for `|`, or
the descriptor-expected reading of a leading `.`. Closing that is closing the
gap in the compiler and deleting the scanner — not porting it. gap[064].
