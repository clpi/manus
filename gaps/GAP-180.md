# GAP-180 — semantic lower-bound certificates

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** !2026-08-18T15:15:00Z
**Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** research_gap
**Filed by:** devin-semantic-20260818
**Files:** docs/spec/constitution.md, docs/spec/law.md, src/perf.id, src/lower_bound.md

For every hot semantic region, derive a certificate of unavoidable work:
required distinctions, minimum information reads, minimum bytes moved, minimum
writes, critical dependency depth, minimum synchronization. Compare the actual
realization against that floor and expose `distance_to_floor(region)` as an
optimization metric.

## Current measured boundary

FTCFTW is currently benchmark-driven. There is no machine-checked lower-bound
infrastructure that proves a realization cannot do less.

**Crossed:**

- `FRONTIER.md` records the canonical priority ladder and rung reach.
- Demand quotienting already exposes how many distinctions a result requires.

**Still missing:**

- Lower-bound facts attached to semantic regions: information, communication,
  computation, memory, synchronization.
- Comparison of selected realization against the proven lower bound.
- `distance_to_floor` as an optimization objective.
- Certificates for: membership must distinguish N possibilities, sorting needs
  `log₂ N!` comparisons, distributed data must cross a partition, etc.
- Information-theoretic and communication-complexity reasoning as graph facts.

## Required order

1. Define `lower_bound(region, dimension)` as a semantic fact.
2. Add dimension families: information, reads, writes, bytes moved, depth,
   synchronization, comparisons.
3. Compute bounds from the demanded quotient, dependencies, and placement.
4. Compare realized cost to the bound and surface the gap.
5. Use the gap to direct optimization search at the highest-ROI region.

## Closure acceptance

Close when hot regions carry proven lower-bound certificates, when the
optimizer can display `distance_to_floor` for each, and when that distance
drives the search toward the largest remaining gap.
