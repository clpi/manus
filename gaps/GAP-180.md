2. Add dimension families: information, reads, writes, bytes moved, depth,
   synchronization, comparisons.
3. Compute bounds from the demanded quotient, dependencies, and placement.
4. Compare realized cost to the bound and surface the gap.
5. Use the gap to direct optimization search at the highest-ROI region.

- **constitutional owners:** witness · provenance · realization (bounds qualify a realization and its problem)

- **semantic invariant:** `lower_bound(region, dimension)` is a witnessed fact over information, reads, writes, bytes moved, depth, synchronization, and comparisons; bounds never establish law, they bound it.
- **physical mechanism:** bound computation from the demanded quotient, dependencies, and placement; `distance_to_floor(region)` comparison.
- **prerequisites:** GAP-176, GAP-178.
- **foreign implication:** communication lower bounds across foreign boundaries constrain fusion (GAP-183).
- **world implication:** bounds are world/target-qualified; every bound states its floor level (F0–F9), never a bare "at floor".
- **projection implication:** none.
- **demand implication:** bounds derive from the demanded quotient — `log₂(distinctions)`.
- **realization implication:** bounds tell the optimizer when further asymptotic search is plausibly futile and direct search at the largest gap; algorithmic/representation/communication/machine gaps reported separately.
- **evidence:** one hot region with a proven bound and a measured `distance_to_floor`.
- **deletion witness:** benchmark-only optimality claims; unsigned floor assertions.
