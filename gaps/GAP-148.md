Reusing a spilled register therefore creates two owners and has a proven silent-miscompile history; the current guard correctly fails closed. WP-03 is false evidence: v1..v20 are unread, so its required STR/LDR canonizes storage that semantic demand does not require. Current bounded backend repair omits only unlineaged unread i64 immediate stores, proves exact assembly/object equality to the equivalent two-binding program, and retains a genuine 20-live-value RegisterExhausted control. Closure requires Idol to assign authoritative graph values to `register` or `frame` realization facts before emission, with CFG/backedge-aware liveness, a prologue-reserved frame, materialization returning the actual register, per-function state reset, value differentials under calls/branches/records, and replacement of the stale spill proof projections.
