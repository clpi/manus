| 1 | powt in lib/compiler/opt/licm.id (called from splitconst) and powtwo in lib/compiler/native.id (called from mulred) and lib/compiler/elfx86.id double p until p >= v to test whether v is a power of two. |
| 2 | For v > 2^62 the doubling overflows i64 (2^62 -> -2^63 -> 0) and 0 < v then holds forever: the loop never terminates. Both hanging constants exceed 2^62; every non-hanging constant is below it. |
| 3 | Confirmed by sampling the hung nativebench: 790/790 samples in idol_compiler_opt_licm__powt, called from idol_compiler_opt_licm__splitconst. |

| section |
|---|---|
| Fix |

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | Guard (if v > 4611686018427387904 return 0 - 1) at the top of all three functions. No power of two above 2^62 fits in i64, so -1 is the exact answer, not a fallback. |
| 2 | Termination: for 2 <= v <= 2^62, p starts at 1 and strictly doubles with no possible overflow (p <= 2^61 before each doubling), so p >= v after at most 62 iterations. |
| 3 | Callers already treat -1 as not-a-power-of-two and fall back to plain mul (mulred) or constant splitting (licm splitconst). No behavior change for any input that previously terminated. |
