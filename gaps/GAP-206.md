## The chain, reproduced out of repo

A probe linking `keyword_classify.c` and `lexer_tokenize.c` as `build.zig` does,
over the fixture `n = 8; r = 0; if (n > 3) …`:

```
PLACE CENSUS:  count=0  points=5   find("n")=false  find("r")=false
REGION CENSUS: count=3  points=5   subject=unknown  lower=unknown
```

`place.zig` `candidateShape` returns `.unknown` for a scalar initializer, so
`bindPlace`'s `if (shape == .unknown) return;` mints no row; `region.zig`
`placeOf` calls `ctx.places.find(name)`, gets null, and `domainOf` falls to an
all-default `Refinement`; the test reads `.at` on a `.unknown` bound and aborts.
The same null empties `collectCarried`, which is the `:815` failure.

`f5857e0a` (2026-08-19, +370/-1262 in one file) established "scalar values,
parameters and homes are not places". `region.zig` was authored in `0c51fc58`,
**306 commits earlier**, against a census that did mint them.

## Two proposals refuted before proposing a third

**Mint scalar places.** Measured in a full mirror: clears all six region rows and
breaks `place: scalar values, parameters and homes are not places` — the exact
invariant `f5857e0a` exists to establish. It also violates the stated intent of
`gate/semantic-graph-authority.sh`, which is in CI and `lacks`-guards
`break :blk .scalar` / `shape = .parameter` / `shape = .home`.

**Retype `Refinement.subject` to the "unused" `region.Site`.** Its premise is
false. `region.Site` is the type of `Region.parent` (`:155`), `Region.target`
(`:160`), `Ctx.loop` (`:219`) and the `parent` parameters of `walkBlock` /
`walkStmt` / `nested`. Its own doc says it exists so "a region id can never be
read as a graph entity id or a place id" — putting a subject id in it is the
conflation it was spelled to prevent. The proposal is also incomplete:
`Bound.place`, `Bound.under` and `Region.carried` carry place ids too, so
retyping `subject` alone fixes four rows of six.

**A graph fact instead.** Measured: none exists. Lifting the fixture through
`liftModuleWithCheckedCalls` yields two nodes, module and func, and
`places=0 origins=0`. `.local` nodes are minted only for `.record`/`.named` type
exprs or checked pack-adjustment binding, and every graph route to a named local
funnels through the same dead census.

## Refuted: the graph could not name a bound scalar

Measured at `b3987e75`. `i64` IS a `.named` TypeExpr, so the graph already mints
a `.local` entity for a TYPED scalar binding:

| fixture | stmt kind | nodes | node named `n` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `n = 8` | `assign` | 2 | 0 |
| `n: i64 = 8` | `local_decl` | 3 | 1 (`kind=local`) |
| `a, b = pair(7,9)` | `assign` | 15 | 0 |

The defect was never a missing identity. `liftBindingsInStmts` already walks
every statement of every relation body; it returns early on `.inferred` and has
NO `.assign` arm, so three spellings of one binding gave two answers — `law.md`
§1, one producer per fact.

The binding census first landed in this gap was a fourth identity space for a
thing `law.md` §2 assigns to the graph ("homes, BINDINGS, reach, worlds,
authority, witnesses") and `law.binding.place` separates from storage. It is
DELETED, not deprecated. Its own witness sentence was also wrong: on
`s = (1,2,3); n = 8` the place and binding spaces AGREE at index 0 (both name
`s`); the collision needs the reverse order.

## Landed: the graph mints the binding

Producer: `semantic_graph.noteLocalBinding`, called from `liftBindingsInStmts`
for `.local_decl`, `.const_decl`, `.global_decl`, `.assign` bare-name targets
and `num_for`/`gen_for` variables, deduplicated against the relation's own
`.local`/`.param` children.

Consumer: `region.Refinement.subject` is `semantic_identity.Card` — the graph's
OWN three-valued reference — and `Bound.place`, `Bound.under` and
`Region.carried` are `semantic_identity.id`. `region.analyzeFunction` DROPS its
place-census parameter: region no longer reads the place census at all. That is
the structural proof the space was rival rather than physical.

Deleted: `place.Binding`, `place.BindingSite`, `Census.bindings`,
`Census.bindingCount`, `Census.findBinding`, `noteBinding`.

## Next seam — NOT closed here

PLACE id through `noteOrigin`'s `body.places.find`, which declines every scalar
(measured `origins=0` on four scalar fixtures; positive control `origins=1` on a

which point `Origin` and `Refinement.subject` are the same space.

## Historical: the two proposals refuted before the census, kept as record

The census that this section replaces removed three failures and all three
crashes with zero regressions, and that measurement stood. It was the wrong
shape, and it was landed without the deletion condition law requires. The two
proposals it had refuted remain refuted: minting scalar places measured +1
regression and breaks the invariant `f5857e0a` exists to establish, and
retyping `subject` to `region.Site` conflates the space `region.Site` was
spelled to keep apart while fixing four rows of six.
