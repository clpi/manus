## The three that matter most

**`gate/all.sh` — `AGENTS.md` gives it to the reader as a command to run.**
A new agent following the bootstrap document runs a file that has never
existed. `AGENTS.md`'s own section *"Numbers live in exactly one place, and
that place runs"* is violated by `AGENTS.md`.

**`gate/narrow.sh` is cited as the authority for a live production refusal**
in `dnir_lower.zig` — *"Deleting this check admits 36 oracle rows that answer
wrong."* The 36 rows have no reproducible artifact.

**`gate/control.sh`** is cited by `region.zig` three times including
*"gate/control.sh measures the hole this closes, and the measurement is the
reason"*, with a digest `ebede795efad` that survives only as prose.

## Why this is P0 and not tidying

A citation to a non-existent gate is indistinguishable, to a reader, from a
citation to a passing one. Every refusal, invariant and design decision resting
on one of these 88 citations is unfalsifiable: there is no artifact to run, no
number to reproduce, and no way to learn that the claim was never measured.
This session filed four wrong answers (GAP-207 through GAP-210) that every
*existing* instrument missed; a ghost instrument cannot even miss them.

## Acceptance

Each of the 28 either
1. gets written and wired, with its cited number reproduced; or
2. has the claim it supports re-measured and the citation replaced by the
   artifact that actually establishes it; or
3. has the citation deleted and the claim marked unproven.

`gate/all.sh` is first, because `AGENTS.md` instructs every new agent to run it.
