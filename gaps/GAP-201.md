## Current exact debt

### Occurrence and application identity

- `OccurrenceBridge` joins applications to expressions by `*const Expr`.
- `applicationFace` and related diagnostics reconstruct relation faces by casting
  `Node.ast_ref` back to AST expressions.
- AST-driven realization walkers recover application ids from provenance rather
  than receiving exact graph occurrence ids in work items.
- graphless convenience lowering strips orphan handles after the fact instead of
  making graph ownership and id lifetime explicit at the API boundary.
- `native_bootstrap.zig` still recognizes bootstrap application meaning from
  source names/roots/method strings and is consulted by graph/lowering gates.

### Projection, world and place identity

- general call-to-index rewriting is gone, but the bounded ambient OS compatibility
  pass still recognizes world members from source spelling before exact graph
  world-member identities exist;
- aggregate place candidates are correct in kind, but are still keyed by names
  and AST binding/init provenance;
- exact root-place, projection/subplace, read/write occurrence and provenance ids
  are not published once and carried through all consumers;
- parser, sema, graph, direct, C, Wasm and tooling candidate sets are not yet one
  shared graph query.

### Name/path/string reconstruction

- name-keyed place lookup and AST initializer provenance;
- path/home/symbol reconstruction after exact entity resolution;
- string-matched worlds, ambient roots, builtin families, conversions, egress,
  memory/string/math/OS operations;
- callee spelling used to infer ABI/result class where graph descriptors exist or
  should exist.

### Descriptor, result, demand and effect duplication

- local predicates such as string/integer/bool/f64/result/record classifiers;
- separate parameter/result ABI maps reconstructed by export name;
- syntax-shaped effect and void/result-demand classification;
- backend eligibility decided by local enums rather than graph-owned laws and
  exact target facts;
- `ast.FuncBody` still owns optimizer/codegen eligibility and result payloads such
  as `use_simple_loop_fold` and related transformation state.

### Transformation lineage

- lowering performs folds, tail rewrites, vector reductions, bounds/trap
  insertion, representation decisions and other semantic transformations without
  graph transformation ids and witnesses;
- occurrence/value/result lineage is repaired retrospectively instead of derived
  from exact replacement edges;
- retained quotient, recurrence, guarded-termination and observer research cannot
  be safely reactivated until this lineage exists.

### DNIR semantic leakage

- DNIR op/tag/field conventions carry vector, trap, bounds, builtin, world or
  transformation meaning instead of only target realization encoding;
- physical operands and semantic facts are not cleanly separated at every API;
- direct, C and Wasm backends still contain local semantic candidate logic.

### Consumer and authority duplication

- grammar/formatter/Tree-sitter/LSP/MCP projections still contain local spelling
  or role knowledge in places;
- consumer-zero or weakly consumed research modules remain in production paths;
- direct, C, Wasm and tool paths do not consume exactly one closed graph
  representation for every admitted construct;
- native/self-host projections require an exact-current upstream pin and executed
  agreement gate after each authority change.

## Required vertical order

1. **Occurrence work items:** graph publishes exact application/value ids into
   resolver/lowering work items; delete AST-pointer joins and orphan-handle
   stripping.
2. **World/application closure:** graph publishes exact ambient/foreign/bootstrap
   relation ids and witnesses; delete name/root/method recognition and the inert
   table-ingress type-map API.
3. **Exact access/place ids:** graph-owned projection/access occurrence ids,
   root-place/subplace facts, determinacy, alias, escape, lifetime, extent and
   residency; delete name-keyed place authority while preserving the landed
   aggregate-only kind boundary.
4. **Descriptor/result/effect facts:** one producer supplies operand/result packs,
   descriptors, effects, world demands and ABI facts to every backend.
5. **Transformation lineage:** every fold, quotient, contraction, early exit,
   vectorization, fusion or layout change is a graph transformation with witness
   and occurrence/value/machine lineage.
6. **Physical-only DNIR:** DNIR carries graph ids plus target realization choices
   and physical operands; semantic local taxonomies monotonically reach zero.
7. **One backend/tool contract:** direct, C, Wasm, JIT/interpreter, LSP, MCP and
   evidence consume the same graph semantics and differ only in physical
   realization/projection.
8. **Performance exploitation:** rerun retained algorithmic and machine research
   on the closed graph; integrate only Pareto wins or physical optima and expose
   every same-algorithm loss.

## Permanent negative controls

- two source faces with one witnessed meaning share semantic identity while
  retaining distinct provenance;
- application and computed projection never share occurrence identity merely
  because a compatibility pass can rewrite text;
- local/parameter bindings shadow ambient worlds without process-global state or
  string-name exceptions downstream;
- scalars, parameters and homes never become places because an implementation
  stores or names them;
- unknown graph facts refuse or conservatively fall back, never infer from
  opcodes, paths, spelling, declaration/load order or host type;
- direct and C backends produce byte/behavior-equivalent observations for the
  admitted subset, including traps, effects and ordering;
- graphless APIs cannot return handles whose owner has been destroyed;
- concurrent compilation cannot cross-wire objects, graph ids, diagnostics or
  cache entries;
- deleting any required semantic fact producer makes a focused gate fail;
- every gate examining zero subjects fails rather than reporting green.

## Performance acceptance

Each vertical slice reports separately:

1. semantic/algorithmic work removed;
2. same-algorithm machine-code quality;
3. compiler/analysis work and memory;
4. startup, artifact, import and relocation cost;
5. applicability over ordinary corpus and constructed controls;
6. exact refusals and no-gain cases.

The landed source deletions reduce classifier/census work by construction, but no
runtime, compile-time or memory number is claimed without an exact clean-head
run. A research speedup on constructed inputs does not close this gap without
current-head applicability and machine evidence. A semantic cleanup with no
activated performance mechanism may land only with no measured regression on the
strongest executable baseline.

## Closure acceptance

Close only when:

- every production application, projection, place, result, effect and
  transformation reaches machine/evidence through exact graph ids with no
  AST/name/path/string semantic reconstruction;
- DNIR is demonstrably physical-only and regenerable from graph facts;
- all backend/tool consumers agree by construction rather than duplicated tests;
- retained FTCFTW mechanisms are remeasured on the exact clean closed-graph head;
- compiler B/SHC and Wasm end-to-end status are reported independently and
  honestly;
- ablation of any required graph fact turns a focused gate red.
