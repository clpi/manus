# GAP-176 — information and demand as quotient semantics

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** !2026-08-18T14:50:00Z
**Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** research_gap
**Filed by:** devin-semantic-20260818
**Files:** docs/spec/constitution.md, docs/spec/law.md, docs/demand-quotient.md, src/sema.zig, src/dnir_lower.zig

Generalize demand beyond "is this value used?" to "which equivalence classes
must remain distinguishable under the admitted observer." Replace ad hoc
notions of projection, quotient, approximation, lossy conversion, and privacy
with a single information order `A ⪰ B` over semantic values.

## Current measured boundary

The quotient/demand machinery already collapses exact state spaces by bit/field
observability. It is not yet a general information-theoretic fact family over
every semantic value. Conversion classes, demand, and approximation remain
separate subsystems.

**Crossed:**

- `demand` exists as a graph fact and drives optimization.
- Periodicity work demonstrates quotienting by demanded bits.

**Still missing:**

- An `information` lattice per value: `full value ⪰ hash`, `text ⪰ length`,
  `f64 ⪰ rounded f32`, `table ⪰ membership`, `value ⪰ sign`.
- Demand as a quotient `Q(value)` rather than a yes/no use flag.
- Standard observer quotients: `length`, `sign`, `cardinality`, `membership`,
  `topk`, `prefix`, `low n bits`, `order`, `shape`, `distribution`.
- `preserve no more information than the observer can distinguish` as a
  realization constraint.
- Integration with conversion safety, serialization, privacy, and approximation.

## Required order

1. Define `information order` in C0: `A ⪰ B` means `A` distinguishes at least
   every difference `B` distinguishes.
2. Add `quotient` as a semantic descriptor fact: the observer demands `Q(value)`.
3. Express `projection`, `conversion`, `demand`, and `approximation` as
   instances of the information order.
4. Make optimization legality `Q(original) = Q(candidate)` for demand `Q`.
5. Compute lower bounds from the demanded quotient: `log₂(distinctions)`
   for information-theoretic minima; communication complexity for data
   movement minima.

## Closure acceptance

Close when `demand` is uniformly an observer quotient, when conversions are
ordered by information loss, and when the optimizer can prove legality from
`Q(original) = Q(candidate)` across all transformations.
