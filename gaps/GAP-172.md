8:## Obligation
9:
10:Stop encoding FTCFTW as an enumerated optimization checklist. Build the
11:machinery that makes every semantic fact, demand, law, physical alternative,
12:proof method, and target resource enter **one measurable frontier**, and makes
13:every uncovered region mechanically visible as **FTCFTW debt**. Individual
14:optimizations are instances discovered inside the frontier (`law.optimization.open`,
15:`law.optimization.space`), never constitution additions.

17:Authority: `docs/spec/law.md` §98–§110 · C0 `law.ftcftw.dominance`,

21:## Acceptance contract
22:
23:```text
24:FTCFTW(case) = semantics-equivalent
25:             AND (Idol dominates best-known competitor
26:                  OR Idol reaches the proven lower bound)
27:```
28:
29:Every case persists exactly one row:
30:
31:```text
32:subject world target workload observations competitor lowerbound idol
33:delta cause producer consumer proof evidence status
34:```
35:
36:Status is a closed enum — **no other value is admissible**:
37:
38:| status | meaning |
39:|---|---|
40:| `win` | strictly dominates the best competitor, with equivalence + confidence |
41:| `bound` | equals the proven lower bound (no improvement physically possible) |
42:| `open` | loses an improvable dimension (carries exact debt row) |
43:| `unknownbound` | no useful lower bound known — NOT optimal, distinct from `bound` |
44:
45:Forbidden framings (never statuses, never closure): `averagewin`, `geomeanwin`,
46:`goodenough`, a collapsed scalar, a geometric mean that hides an open loss.
47:Idol opens a case **automatically** whenever it loses an improvable dimension.
48:
49:## Five graphs carry the frontier (A–E)
50:
51:| graph | shape |
52:|---|---|
53:| **A observation** | application/use → required observations → producer → proof |
54:| **B demand** | use → demanded property/portion/quality → upstream transformer |
55:| **C law** | relation/descriptor → semantic law → witness → implications |
56:| **D frontier** | region → candidate realization → preconditions → proof → cost vector → dependencies → status |
57:| **E debt** | region → lower bound → selected realization → delta → cause |
58:
59:`E.cause` is classified, not prose: `missing-fact` · `missing-demand` ·
60:`missing-law` · `missing-candidate` · `missing-search` · `missing-proof` ·
61:`wrong-cost-model` · `wrong-placement` · `wrong-algorithm` ·
62:`wrong-representation`.

64:## Mechanical development loop
65:
66:```text
67:measure → find largest weighted debt → classify (E.cause)
68:        → implement → validate (law.optimization.validated) → ratchet
69:```

71:A semantic opportunity with **no candidate provider** is debt made visible
72:*before* a benchmark regression (search-space gap: e.g. GPU present but no GPU
73:candidate producer; associativity known but no reduction-search provider; static
74:keys known but no perfect-hash candidate).
