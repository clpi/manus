# GAP-171 — Semantic basis: the foundational algebras (square-zero)

**Status:** OPEN · **Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** language design / semantics
**Owner:** Cursor (canonicality / design authority) · consumers: Codex (graph), Poolside (realization)

## Obligation

Design the complete mathematical contract from which FTCFTW *follows by
construction* rather than as an ever-growing checklist. Law: `docs/spec/law.md`
§104 OBSERVATION-ONE, §105 BOUNDARY-ONE, §106 foundational algebras,
§107 OPTIMIZATION-SPACE-COMPLETE, §108 REALIZATION-CONTRACT; C0
`law.observation.one`, `law.equivalence.observation`, `law.demand.derivative`,
`law.relation.property`, `law.change.delta`, `law.uncertainty.algebra`,
`law.optimizer.economy`, `law.optimization.space`, `law.realization.contract`.
Catalogue: `docs/performance.md` § Square-zero foundational basis. The
observation model itself is GAP-170.

The frontier is the whole realization set — corrected by §108 REALIZATION-CONTRACT
to the full tuple `R(S,O,W,D,K,E,H,P,F,B)` of every physically lawful realization
whose observations lie in `allowed(O,S,W,F)`, ranging over computation *and
noncomputation*. FTCFTW = search `R` → verified Pareto frontier → dominate every
known competitor → equal the proven lower bound (naming which bound). A candidate
is admitted iff it (1) preserves demanded observations under the current world,
(2) satisfies authority/effect/resource/fault/security constraints, (3) is
verifiable, and (4) improves the chosen frontier. Every individual optimization
(the ~200 classes in `docs/performance.md`) is an instance discovered *inside*
`R` on the ~24 axes (identity, observation, law, knowledge, uncertainty, demand,
change, equivalence, information, work, communication, representation,
architecture, placement, schedule, boundary, failure, resource, search,
verification, evidence, cost, adaptation, meta-cost) — never a new constitutional
mechanism.

Lower bounds are **information-theoretic and physical**, not algorithmic:
information required vs processed, communication complexity across every boundary,
I/O and cache-block complexity, circuit depth/size, parallel work vs span,
synchronization/round complexity, roofline, and ultimately speed-of-light /
memory-latency / thermodynamic-energy limits. **Lawful nonexecution** (cached
answer, theorem, materialized state, world-fact preanswer, demand/observer
elimination) is the ultimate realization. **Architecture is realization**
(boundary erasure and introduction; cross-layer optimization). **Meta-cost is
lifecycle-global** (break-even execution count; distributional costs).

Define the smallest observable semantics; everything not observable is
realization space; continuously search that space for the least-cost verified
realization. The strongest statement: semantic observations are the only
invariants; identity and facts define which observations are required; everything
else may disappear, transform, migrate, or be synthesized under verified
equivalence.

The frontier `R` is over the full parameter tuple `R(S,O,W,D,K,E,H,P,F,B)` (§107
corrected shape) and factors into **24 foundational axes**: identity, observation,
law, knowledge, uncertainty, demand, change, equivalence, information, work,
communication, representation, architecture, placement, schedule, boundary,
failure, resource, search, verification, evidence, cost, adaptation, meta-cost.
The six algebras below are the semantic core of that set; the information / work /
communication / architecture / failure / meta-cost axes are the physical and
economic extensions carried in GAP-169 and `law.lower.bound` / `law.optimizer.economy`.
"Better" is defined by lower-bound *families* (information-theoretic, communication,
I/O, circuit, work/span, physical law), not instruction counts, and cost is a
distribution (no dominance with overlapping confidence; meta-cost lifecycle-global).

## Algebras to design (graph-level facts + producers)

1. **Equivalence** (`law.equivalence.observation`) — a formal equivalence over
   results, failure, effects, authority, ordering, observable identity, and
   resource obligations; the substrate for translation validation and open search.
   Keep identity equality vs semantic equivalence vs representation sharing distinct.
2. **Demand derivative** (`law.demand.derivative`) — a general
   `demand(output) → demand(inputs)` transformer carrying portion/quality/
   cardinality/order; partial consumption, existential/`any`/`all`/`count`/
   membership, top-k/order-statistic, aggregate-demand rewrites, and inverse demand.
   Bidirectional demand↔facts↔realization fixed point with cycle handling,
   termination, and widening for open worlds.
3. **Relation-law algebra** (`law.relation.property`) — associativity,
   commutativity, identity, idempotence, invertibility, monotonicity,
   distributivity, fusion-compatibility, descriptor subsumption, and a law-
   implication graph, registered as relation facts (not arithmetic special-casing).
4. **Change/delta** (`law.change.delta`) — semantic derivative/delta for
   incrementalization, generalized differentiation (incrementalization, gradients,
   dependency updates), and reversibility.
5. **Uncertainty** (`law.uncertainty.algebra`) — the refinement lattice; strict
   fact/evidence/assumption/proof separation; first-class partial knowledge; a
   general absence algebra; and distinct unknown/ambiguity/contradiction states
   with provenance.
6. **Optimizer economy** (`law.optimizer.economy`) — ROI-budgeted search/proof,
   proof-carrying realization cache, anytime/background/distributed search,
   discovery-vs-trusted-admission separation, minimized trusted kernel, and
   verified-rewrite promotion (self-improvement).

## Interactions

- Numeric/precision (GAP-169 precision class): numeric domain (integer/real/
  bounded/modular/exact/approximate/fixed-point/probability/angle/index) is
  separate from representation width; overflow (mathematical/checked/modular/
  saturating/trapping) and FP facts (strict IEEE/finite/no-NaN/no-signed-zero/
  bounded-error/associative-permitted) are relation/descriptor facts; approximation
  is a demand dimension yielding an equivalence class, not one bit pattern.
- Collections: order is separate from container; locality is separate from logical
  structure; consistency model (SC/causal/release-acquire/eventual/snapshot),
  atomic/transactional observation, effect commutativity/idempotence, and
  contention layout are effect constraints, not mandatory objects.
- Sealing/closure: closed-vs-open world is multi-dimensional (bindings, relation
  definitions, metatables, world members, descriptor facts, foreign environment);
  mutation epochs/version facts drive exact specialization invalidation and
  deopt-as-continuation (interpreter is one physical recovery, not required).
- Compilation itself is an ordinary Idol program over worlds (source world →
  semantic graph → target world → realization); "compiler pass" is a physical
  scheduling unit only.
- Faults/reliability/security: fault model, reliability, and side-channel
  observability are world-dependent and join the Pareto frontier (loops back to
  GAP-170 world-dependent observation sets).
- Physical lower bounds (§108): information, communication, I/O/cache-block,
  circuit, work-vs-span, synchronization/round, roofline, and physical-law limits
  are the cost floor `law.lower.bound` measures against — a case is not FTCFTW-closed
  until it names which bound was reached. Consumer: GAP-152 aggregate, GAP-169.
- Lawful nonexecution and architecture-as-realization (§108): demand elimination,
  observer elimination, world-fact preanswer, content-addressed result reuse, and
  boundary erasure/introduction are realizations `R` must be able to select, not
  out-of-scope systems concerns.

## Floor (2026-08-13)

**IMPLEMENTATION-BLOCKED at S0.** No compiler B; the graph carries none of these
algebras yet. This is design + law authority, not measured capability. Each
algebra becomes actionable only when the graph can produce and consume its facts
and the verifier can admit candidates (`law.optimization.validated`).

## Deletion conditions

1. Each algebra above has an explicit graph-fact representation with a single
   producer (`law.fact.producer.one`) and demand-driven consumers.
2. The equivalence checker admits/refuses candidate realizations against a shared
   observation specification (GAP-170), with proof or validation obligation.
3. The demand-derivative transformer drives at least one end-to-end asymptotic win
   (e.g. `sort:first()` → selection, `map:sum()` → no materialization) proven by
   `law.optimization.validated` and measured by `law.lower.bound`.
4. Optimizer economy budgets real search and reuses a proof-carrying cache, with a
   minimized trusted core distinct from discovery.

## Nine-universe structural closure (2026-08-13)

The foundational algebras are the semantic core of a **nine-universe** closure
(`docs/spec/law.md` §110 NINE-UNIVERSE, C0 `law.realization.universe`): meaning ·
observation · knowledge · demand · equivalence · realization · resource ·
search+proof · change. This GAP owns the semantic-core universes (equivalence,
demand, relation-law, change, uncertainty, optimizer-economy); the physical and
economic universes are carried by GAP-169/170. Design obligations added by the
closure:

- the equivalence checker must preserve demanded **hyperproperties** (sets of
  executions: determinism, noninterference, serializability, linearizability),
  not only single-trace observations (GAP-170, `law.obligation.one`);
- **value-of-information** is an optimization action — the cost model must be
  able to schedule fact acquisition (profile/probe/prescan/query/agent contract)
  by `cost-to-learn` vs `expected savings`;
- **contract-weakening / semantic-debt** — an unobserved declared guarantee is
  measurable debt whose removal expands the realization set;
- **monotonic frontier** — a historical best-realization and best-competitor
  archive per (workload, target, world); a newer compiler must beat or reproduce
  each closed Pareto point, and knowing regression is first-class debt;
- **optimality gap** = `best-known − proven-lower-bound`; keep "none faster
  found" distinct from "proven none faster exists" (`unbounded/unknown` status).

Completeness is **structural, not enumerative**: no future capability mints a
parallel algebra; it enriches these identities.
