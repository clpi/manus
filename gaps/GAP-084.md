## The reading

The constitution says six primitives suffice and everything else is a *family of
facts*. The bootstrap carries **293 mechanism-bearing nouns** against those six.

That ratio is not itself the finding — a bootstrap in a host language will
always have more nouns than the law it implements. The finding is **which**
nouns, and that they are not host necessities:

- `ProtocolRegistry` is not required by Zig. Zig requires a map; the *protocol
  registry* concept is a design choice on top of one.
- `DeriveRegistry` names a relation the constitution already names.
- The `prove*Registry` family — nine of them — are catalogs of facts, which is
  the definition of the `fact` primitive.

`law.host.projection` grants un-idol-like structure ONLY where the host requires
it. A `std.AutoHashMap` is required. A noun that duplicates a constitutional
primitive is not.

## Why this is the highest-leverage remaining measurement

MCR multiplies where the others add. SER (gap[082]) and RSR (gap[084a], below)
each measure ONE dimension's leverage. MCR measures whether the *dimensions
themselves* stop multiplying — and the constitution's claim is that they do,
because relations compose where subsystems do not.

Each of these 64 nouns is a candidate for the challenge in
`law.arch.relation`: **is this distinction recoverable from operands, levels,
homes, lawsets or context?** If yes, the noun is bootstrap debt with a deletion
gate. If no, it is a genuine mechanism and the constitution's list of six is
incomplete — **which would be a finding about the constitution**, and worth more
than the repair.

## Companion measurement — RSR is 1

*Realized Specialization Ratio: distinct optimized machine realizations per
authored semantic body.* The design target is combinatorial — one kernel
yielding thousands of realizations across descriptor × ISA × layout × ownership.

Measured: an untyped `twice = (x) x + x` called at both `i64` and `f64` emits
**zero specialized definitions** — it goes boxed, one dynamic path. A typed body
emits exactly one.

**RSR = 1.** One authored body, one realization. Together with **SER = 1**
(gap[082]), both metrics that "prove idol is actually delivering" currently
read 1, and both are honest baselines rather than failures — nothing has claimed
otherwise, and now nothing can.
