## The reading

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | The constitution says six primitives suffice and everything else is a *family of facts*. |
| 2 | The bootstrap carries **293 mechanism-bearing nouns** against those six. |

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | That ratio is not itself the finding — a bootstrap in a host language will always have more nouns than the law it implements. |
| 2 | The finding is **which** nouns, and that they are not host necessities: |

- `ProtocolRegistry` is not required by Zig. Zig requires a map; the *protocol
  registry* concept is a design choice on top of one.
- `DeriveRegistry` names a relation the constitution already names.
- The `prove*Registry` family — nine of them — are catalogs of facts, which is
  the definition of the `fact` primitive.

| # | directive |
|---|---|
| 1 | `law.host.projection` grants un-idol-like structure ONLY where the host requires it. |
| 2 | A `std.AutoHashMap` is required. |
| 3 | A noun that duplicates a constitutional primitive is not. |

   already names. They are the two that make the argument.
4. **Publish MCR with its denominator**, exactly as SER needs its N: "293
   mechanisms against 6 primitives" is a measurement; "49× compression" without
