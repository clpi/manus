| # | directive |

| 1 | `@(expr)` is comptime evaluation and `{ a = 1 }` is an ordinary pack, so `@({ a = 1 })` is comptime evaluation OF a pack — an ordinary, legal composition of two live faces. |
| 2 | The printer drops the parens because it believed the brace was itself the delimiter of a third construct. |
