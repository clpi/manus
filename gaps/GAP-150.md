## Obligation

Constitution §43 `law.brace` requires an ordinary callable to use parentheses.
A braced application whose subject resolves in callable space must diagnose at
resolution, before argument-pack checking, and must never reach C or Lua
fallback. The valid sibling faces remain ordinary parenthesized application and
braced descriptor application.

## Closure

The production refusal remains immediately after subject resolution and before
argument traversal. Sema now retains its rendered error facts per compilation,
with explicit `complete` or `incomplete` evidence when recording allocation
fails. All Sema-local error emission converges through that producer; terminal
output is a rendering of the retained message, not the only evidence copy.
Owned messages and storage are released by `Sema.deinit`, and successful
compilations allocate no diagnostic entries.

The blocking test reads the tracked canonical fixture
`examples/compile_fail/callable_braces.id`, requires exactly one complete
diagnostic, and compares the message actually produced by checking that fixture
with the exact `law.brace` refusal. Reordering the refusal behind pack arity,
changing its text, or producing another error therefore fails the test. No new
`.id` harness or process-global capture was added.
