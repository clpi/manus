# GAP-224 — the declaration freeze fails open on the canonical definition form

**Status:** OPEN
**Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** evidence_gap
**Filed by:** claude-coordinator
**Files:** gate/vocab-extract.awk,gate/vocabulary.sh,gate/idiom.id

## The defect

`gate/vocabulary.sh` is a fail-closed declaration freeze: it refuses every
newly declared non-private module-scope identity, and says "no word-list edit
can waive this". Its extractor does not see the canonical definition form.

Reproduce — the extractor is the subject, so run it directly:

    printf '+pick = (row: str)\n'        | awk -v MODE=diff -f gate/vocab-extract.awk
    printf '+pick(row, kind) = (row)\n'  | awk -v MODE=diff -f gate/vocab-extract.awk

The first answers `relation pick` and is refused by the freeze. **The second
answers nothing.** `name(...) = (...)` — the canonical definition form — is
invisible to the extractor, so a one-word, underscore-free, fully lawful new
module-scope relation lands unnoticed.

That is a fail-open in a gate whose entire purpose is to be fail-closed, and
it is worse than a missing rule: the freeze reports ADMITTED, so the absence
of a finding reads as a decision that none was warranted.

## The second, separate problem: the door it does advertise is unlawful

`gate/vocab-extract.awk:12` defines the escape:

    # PUBLIC is "name does not begin with `_`"

so the sanctioned way past the freeze is a leading underscore. `gate/idiom.id`
refuses that spelling by name (`underscore [LAW-ONE]`).

**Ruling, anchored in the right place.** `docs/spec/law.md` is supreme and
`docs/spec/constitution.md` is its structured expansion, subordinate wherever
they diverge. `law.md` is SILENT on identifier spelling — it rules on
projection, application, namespace and identity, not on the bytes of a name.
Where the supreme law is silent, C0 governs, and C0 is unambiguous:
`underscore = false` (`constitution.md:235`), "native underscore identifiers
are zero" (:5565), and :3113 denies one-wording an underscore as WORSE than
the underscore. `idiom.id` projects C0 faithfully; `vocab-extract.awk`
advertises a spelling C0 forbids.

So the two gates do conflict, and `idiom.id` is the one to keep. But the
conflict is NOT a deadlock — see below.

## What this gap corrected about itself

It was first filed claiming the underscore was the freeze's ONLY door, and
therefore that no lawful new module-scope relation could be declared at all.
**That premise was false**, disproved by the measurement above: the canonical
`name(...) = (...)` form passes the freeze without an underscore and without
conflicting with `idiom.id`. The real finding is the fail-open, which the
"deadlock" reading had hidden.

## Acceptance

1. `gate/vocab-extract.awk` recognizes the canonical `name(...) = (...)`
   definition form. A control: the two commands above must BOTH yield
   `relation pick`.
2. A planted new module-scope relation in canonical form is refused by
   `gate/vocabulary.sh`, proving the freeze is closed rather than quiet.
3. `gate/vocab-extract.awk` stops naming a C0-forbidden spelling as the
   definition of private, and the `_`-prefixed declarations admitted under it
   are enumerated by a runner and given a migration disposition.
4. `gate/idiom.id` is unchanged, or changed only in a direction C0 licenses.
5. Whatever population figures this repair needs are produced by an executable
   enumerator. No count is written into this file: the commands above are the
   authority, and `sh gate/vocabulary.sh --base <rev>` is the verdict.
