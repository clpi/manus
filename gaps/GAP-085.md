# GAP-085 — the ad-hoc trust system already exists, and absence is not expressible

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** 2026-08-08
**Law:** A5 WITNESS (no optimization or claim without an inspectable witness) ·
`law.fact.primitive` · §3 MEASUREMENT HONESTY
**Frontier items:** #1 negative facts / proof of absence · #3 trust/proof
lattice for unverifiable user knowledge

## The warning, and the measurement

The frontier review warns that performance pressure "will eventually create
ad-hoc `unsafe`, `assume`, `likely`, `noalias`, `aligned`, `pure`, `constexpr`
mechanisms all over again" unless one trust/proof lattice covers inferred,
proven, observed, asserted, foreign-guaranteed and guarded facts.

Measured over tracked `src/*.zig`, 2026-08-08. **It is not a future risk.**

| | count |
|---|---:|
| `use_*` assumption flags reaching codegen | **63** |
| `verify_*` companions | **28** |
| **assumptions with NO verifier** | **35** |
| `detect_*` recognizers | 42 |
| `assume` / `noalias` / `is_pure` / `likely` / `unlikely` sites | **51** |

Six distinct ad-hoc trust mechanisms — `use_`, `verify_`, `detect_`, `assume`,
`noalias`, `likely` — none of them a fact, none of them witnessed, none of them
composable, and **35 assumptions with nothing checking them.**

This is exactly the system the frontier review says to avoid building, already
built, and it grew by accretion rather than decision — which is how it escaped
notice.

## Why 35 unverified is the sharp number, not 63

CLAUDE.md §3 already carries this rule, earned the hard way:

> **A detector must verify every constant its emitter assumes**, and decline to
> the general path otherwise. Measured 2026-08-08, **46 `use_*` flags reach
> codegen and 7 carry a `verify_*`**.

The count has since moved to 63/28, so the ratio improved (7/46 = 15% to 28/63 =
44%) while the absolute hole grew (39 to 35, roughly flat). Neither number is
tracked by a gate. §3 states the rule and nothing enforces it.

That section also records what unverified assumptions cost here: ten fabricated
benchmark kernels, three returning frozen literals, and
`use_dot_product_identity` emitting `n(n+1)(n+2)/6` while **verifying nothing
about the fill** — the identical defect that made `detect_binary_search_dense`
answer 200000 where C answered 100000.

## The other half: absence is barely expressible

Optimization depends on negative facts — no alias, no metatable write, no
foreign re-entry, no other reachable implementation, no failure edge, no
observing thread. Measured:

| negative fact spelling | sites |
|---|---:|
| `@{ ambient = false }` | **4** |
| every other negative fact | **0** |

One spelling, four uses, all of it the `std@{ ambient = false }` protocol. There
is no `@{ alias = false }`, no `@{ reentry = false }`, no `@{ failure = false }`.

So the 63 `use_*` flags are what stands in for absence: **the compiler cannot
say "no alias exists," so it says `use_noalias` and hopes.** The two frontier
items are one defect — an unmodelled trust dimension — and the ad-hoc mechanisms
are the shape it takes when the model is missing.

## What a lattice would have to carry

Not six mechanisms. One fact with a trust level, per the review:

```
inferred            the compiler derived it
proven              witnessed, A5 satisfied
observed            profile-time, carries its sample
asserted            the user knows and the compiler cannot check
foreign             a lawset guarantees it, trust-tagged
guarded             true under a runtime check, with its descent
```

`asserted` is the one that matters and the one the tree lacks entirely. There is
no way to say "I know this and you cannot prove it" and have it (a) recorded,
(b) counted, (c) invalidatable, (d) attributable when it turns out false. The 35
unverified `use_*` flags are asserted facts wearing no label, which is why
nobody can enumerate them.

## Fix, in order

1. **A gate over the 63/28 split**, ratcheting the unverified count DOWN.
   §3 states the rule; make it a number. `zig build bootstrap-scan` already
   ratchets three rows over `src/*.zig` and is the natural home.
2. **Name the trust levels as facts** in `constitution.duo`, so `asserted` has
   a spelling and therefore a census.
3. **Negative facts get a spelling** beyond `ambient = false`. `@{ alias =
   false }` is the same form the anchored protocols already use — NNS, no new
   surface (A2).
4. **Every `use_*` becomes a fact with a trust level.** The ones that survive
   are `guarded` or `proven`; the rest are `asserted` and countable.

## The disconfirming case, stated

If the 35 unverified flags turn out individually sound — each one guarded by
something the grep cannot see — then the number is noise and the finding is only
that they are *unenumerable*, which is still worth fixing but is a smaller
claim. **Read ten of them before writing the gate.** §3's own rule applies to
this gap: verify by value, not by count.
