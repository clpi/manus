Fresh value-checked six-runtime measurement on benchmarks/wasm_rt/hot_big.wasm with the actual `zig-out/bin/wasm-conform` and DUO_WASM_* contract: Duon 130 ms, wasmtime 110 ms, wasmer 121 ms, wart 131 ms, wasm3 735 ms, iwasm 713 ms (min of 5 interleaved; answers verified before timing). DUO_WASM_PHASES shows read ~22 us, walks ~2 us, executable allocation ~1 us, JIT compile ~110 us, while engine execution is ~128-134 ms: the 20 ms loss is generated-code execution, not startup or compilation. Machine dump shows the 900M-iteration kernel loop is about 15 ARM64 instructions: two-word materialization for add constant, three alias/local MOVs, two-word multiplier materialization, MUL+ADD, two-word -9 materialization, ADD, MOV, CBNZ. Wasmtime's explored kernel loop is about 9 instructions and uses MADD plus fewer moves. General opportunities are signed-negative add/sub immediate factoring, multiplication-add fusion, and authoritative value liveness/register allocation across local.tee and the loop back dependency.
