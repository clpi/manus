# GAP-185 — world authority to hardware capability realization

**Status:** OPEN · **Filed:** !2026-08-18T15:55:00Z
**Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** research_gap
**Filed by:** devin-semantic-20260818
**Files:** docs/spec/constitution.md, docs/spec/law.md, src/world.zig, src/lower.zig

World/authority facts should select among alternate physical realizations of the
same semantic authority: software check, pointer bounds, MPK, CHERI capability,
process isolation, Wasm sandbox. CHERI demonstrates that hardware capabilities
can enforce source-level pointer authority; Idol should derive capability
construction directly from world facts.

## Current measured boundary

Worlds are semantic contexts. The path from a world/authority fact to an
enforcement mechanism now EXISTS as a bounded decision pair (`src/world.zig`
facts, `src/lower.zig` selection); what remains is the backend consumer during
realization.

**Crossed:**

- `world` and `authority` are C0 fact families.
- `law.world` and `law.capability` exist.
- Required order 1–4 (`09c7d1c38`): the `Mechanism` index, `TargetWorld`
  availability derived from triple facts, the per-mechanism cost relation, and
  CHERI construction derived from `unique`/`nonescape`/`readonly`/extent facts
  — never hand-written.
- Required order 5 through the graph (`5fb4c433`): `lower.selectPlace` is the
  graph-facing consumer — authority facts reach `select` through the census
  the graph carries (`SemanticGraph.places`), accesses are the census's own
  multiplicities, an unknown multiplicity is an unknown workload (never a
  small one), and a statically fixed census place realizes at zero cost.
  Measure: `zig build unit-test` — every `lower:`/`world:` test passes.

**Still missing:**

- A BACKEND consumer that calls `lower.selectPlace` per census place during
  realization — the deletion witness's next host boundary, stated in
  `src/lower.zig`'s header.
- The bounded slice `src/world.zig`'s header names: what a `foreign_boundary`
  world fact does to enforcement admissibility.
- Measured (not estimated) per-mechanism costs once a target exists to measure
  them — `lower.costOf` states orders (`law` §14).

## Required order

1. Define authority realization candidates in C0.
2. Add target-world facts for available hardware mechanisms.
3. Build a cost model for authority enforcement.
4. Derive CHERI/MPK/bounds construction from semantic authority facts.
5. Prove zero overhead when the compiler can show authority is statically fixed.
