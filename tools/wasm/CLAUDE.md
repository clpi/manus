|---|---|

| 3 | It must compare semantic results by value with an identified external oracle, cover each demanded entry and realization shape, and include positive and negative controls. |
| 4 | Transport completion, process exit, non-sentinel output, or one successful realization is not conformance evidence. |

| # | directive |

| 2 | It records the exact revision, dirty state, machine, target, oracle, input, selected realization, fallback/refusal reason, repetitions, and known unsupported or divergent cases. |
| 3 | Report decode/import, compile, instantiate, startup, steady execution, memory, runtime footprint, artifact size, and end-to-end latency separately. |
| 4 | A throughput gain may not hide cold-start, compiler, memory, or artifact regressions. |

| # | directive |
